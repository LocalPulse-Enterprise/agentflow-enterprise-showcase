# Product Overview

AgentFlow Enterprise is an AI RevOps SaaS foundation for lead qualification, secure workflow automation, and buyer-ready technical due diligence.

## What It Does

AgentFlow helps structure the path from a new lead to a qualified business action. The product direction covers lead intake, AI-assisted scoring, operator review, billing readiness, and handoff into a CRM or workflow system.

In plain terms: it helps a team avoid letting valuable inbound interest sit in disconnected forms, inboxes, or manual spreadsheets without clear next steps.

## Why It Matters

Lead response speed and qualification quality directly affect revenue operations. When teams rely on manual review, unclear routing, and disconnected tools, qualified opportunities can be delayed or missed.

AgentFlow is designed to give teams a more structured way to:

- Capture inbound interest
- Evaluate fit with AI assistance
- Review qualified requests
- Prepare billing or next-step actions
- Hand off work into the right revenue process

## Who Benefits

AgentFlow is most relevant for:

- Agencies serving clients with lead qualification or RevOps needs
- SaaS founders validating a productized qualification workflow
- RevOps consultants building repeatable client processes
- Senior developers evaluating a production-conscious SaaS foundation
- Potential acquirers reviewing a post-build, pre-revenue asset

## How the Workflow Works

The public workflow model is:

Lead Intake -> AI Qualification -> Operator Review -> Billing Readiness -> CRM / Workflow Handoff

Each step is meant to reduce ambiguity. The system helps move a lead from raw inbound signal to a more actionable operating state.

## Current Product Position

AgentFlow Enterprise should be understood as a production-conscious SaaS foundation with buyer-readiness documentation. It is not presented as revenue validated, enterprise adopted, or formally certified.

The strongest current value is the combination of product foundation, RevOps use case clarity, public demo direction, and acquisition-ready documentation.

## Why This Public Repository Is Safe and Limited

This repository contains public product documentation only. It is designed to help buyers understand the product without exposing private system knowledge.

It does not include private source code, database structure, API internals, checkout internals, webhook handlers, secrets, or operational procedures.

## Why the Private Codebase Remains Protected

The private codebase contains the implementation details that should be reviewed only in a controlled diligence process. Protecting that information reduces security risk, preserves commercial leverage, and keeps public evaluation focused on product fit rather than operational exposure.
